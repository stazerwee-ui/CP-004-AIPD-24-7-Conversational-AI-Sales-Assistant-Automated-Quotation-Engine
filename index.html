How we handle what you share with us.

PDPA Singapore Compliance Notice · Secure Sanctuary

1. What We Collect

Account details (name, username, email or phone), arrangement selections, deceased loved one's details entered during consultation, messages with our assistant, and digital signature image.

2. Why We Collect This Information

To prepare transparent quotations with guaranteed pricing, to allow our funeral directors to follow up and assist in emergencies, and to let your family resume saved arrangements securely.

3. Conversational Assistant & AI Safety

Messages are processed by an assistant to guide intake step-by-step. Authorized human directors may review conversations to assist. Sensitive identifiers like NRIC/FIN and payment card numbers are masked automatically and are never required.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Records are retained only for as long as required for memorial coordination and Singapore statutory obligations. You may request account closure and draft deletion at any time.

5. Who Sees Your Information

Restricted strictly to authorized Solace directors and care coordinators. Bereavement data is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

6. Your Rights Under the PDPA

You have the right to request access, correction, or withdrawal of consent under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act.

7. Contact

Data Protection Officer: privacy@solace.sg.
